# Twenty-Four Temporal Seats: Research Draft

Status: `research-draft`, not an issuance list.

The project wants to study 24 broad temporal and cultural seats rather than
treat one region as the only art history.

Possible coverage:

- early human material culture;
- ancient river civilizations;
- classical Mediterranean and West Asian traditions;
- South and East Asian traditions;
- African kingdoms and material culture;
- Mesoamerican and Andean traditions;
- Pacific and Oceanian traditions;
- medieval, early modern and industrial transitions;
- modern and contemporary global exchange.

No object is eligible for issuance until its museum or authoritative source,
copyright boundary, image rights and edition policy are published and reviewed.

## What the community should submit

For each proposed seat:

- culture and approximate period;
- representative object or art tradition;
- authoritative public references;
- why the seat improves geographic and temporal balance;
- image and derivative-work rights;
- proposed scarcity and edition cap with reasoning;
- known disputes or provenance uncertainty.

The list must not be described as final before the signed catalog hash is
published.
